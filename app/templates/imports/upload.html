How this works

Bank statement layouts vary a lot, so Budgeteer's parser makes its best guess at the date, description, amount, and whether each line is income or an expense (and tries to match it to one of your existing categories). Nothing is written to your ledger yet - the next page lets you fix, remove, or add to anything before you confirm.

On the review page you can also flag any line as recurring (e.g. a salary deposit or a subscription debit order), which sets it up as a recurring item for the cash flow forecaster too.
